# Social drafts

## The problem

Australian R&D Tax Incentive guidance says records should be created when the activity is conducted. Reconstructing an experiment months later leaves memory to fill the gaps. Rdlog is a focused place to record the hypothesis, method, observation, evaluation and conclusion as the work happens. Record-keeping only; not tax advice or an eligibility decision.

## The demo

Create an experiment. Append an entry. Rdlog SHA-256 hash-links it to the previous record, sends the hash to two OpenTimestamps calendars and stores the exact responses. Corrections remain visible as linked amendments. One JSON export carries the record, chain and receipts: https://s72-rdlog.pages.dev

Fresh receipts remain pending until upgraded and independently verified against Bitcoin.

## Why it is free

Rdlog is free and open source because the first question is whether this record-and-export loop is useful to technical teams and R&D advisers. There are no customer numbers or outcome claims yet. Feedback on the experiment vocabulary and JSON pack is more useful than launch-day applause.
