# Rdlog launch drafts

These are drafts for a human to review, rewrite where a community requires original human-authored text, and post manually. Nothing in this directory has been posted.

## Evidence and audience

The evidence quote comes from [Australian Government record-keeping guidance](https://business.gov.au/grants-and-programs/research-and-development-tax-incentive/check-if-you-are-eligible-for-the-randd-tax-incentive/record-keeping-for-the-rd-tax-incentive), not a subreddit. Do not claim that a Reddit community originated or endorsed the product.

The closest specific Reddit discussion is in [r/AusFinance](https://www.reddit.com/r/AusFinance/), where founders have asked about preparing R&D claims themselves and choosing an adviser, including a discussion that warned against reconstructed evidence. Its current rules prohibit advertising or promotion of a company, site, app or product owned by the poster. The Reddit draft is therefore **not cleared to post**. Use it only if moderators give explicit prior permission.

## Suggested order

1. **Private practitioner review.** Send the live link directly to one Australian R&D tax practitioner or startup finance adviser already known to the owner. Ask them to test the exported structure, not to endorse it. This is private feedback, not a community post.
2. **Show HN.** Only after the live app can be tried without an email gate. [Show HN rules](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html) allow things the maker built that readers can use and require the maker to be present for discussion. Do not solicit votes. The broader [HN guidelines](https://news.ycombinator.com/newsguidelines.html) prohibit generated or AI-edited comments, so the human poster must rewrite the draft in their own words before posting.
3. **Product Hunt.** A maker may post their own product. Use a personal account, not a company account; do not pay a hunter, buy traffic or ask people to upvote. Product Hunt recommends joining the community before launch and currently applies a waiting period to new accounts. Check the [launch guide](https://www.producthunt.com/launch) and [posting guide](https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/479557-how-to-post-a-product) again on launch day.
4. **Owner’s existing social channels.** Share the problem, a short working demo and the free/open-source rationale. Do not imply customers, adoption or government endorsement.
5. **Reddit only with moderator approval.** r/AusFinance’s current self-promotion rule blocks the prepared draft. [r/AustralianStartups rules](https://www.reddit.com/r/AustralianStartups/) also say free apps and FOSS repositories are not suitable, so do not cross-post there. The current rules for r/ausbusiness could not be verified clearly enough to approve a post; ask its moderators before preparing a tailored version.

## Claims to keep out of every post

- Do not call a calendar response a confirmed Bitcoin timestamp until it has been upgraded and independently verified.
- Do not call the hash chain immutable, tamper-proof or regulator proof.
- Do not say Rdlog determines eligibility, replaces an adviser, satisfies an audit, or is endorsed by the ATO or the department.
- Do not invent users, customers, reviews, savings, pricing or outcomes.

The useful first feedback is narrow: whether the record types match how technical teams describe experiments, whether the pending-receipt language is understandable, and whether the JSON export is useful to an adviser.
