# rdlog

Rdlog is a research and development logger that helps you document experiments in real-time.

## Why rdlog?
- **Efficiency**: Save time and reduce complexity in your workflow.
- **Reliability**: Built for consistency and long-term use.
- **Fast setup**: Get up and running in minutes.

[View rdlog on GitHub](https://github.com/FishyDanny/rdlog)

## Marketable Value Proposition
Rdlog brings transparency and accountability to your R&D process, ensuring your experiments are documented securely as they happen.
